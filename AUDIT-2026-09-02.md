## 2. Findings

### F1 — P7's "coverage 16%" is the classical-SE number; Ciccone's recipe reports a robust SE (medium)

`src/stage1_recompute.py` computes an HC1 robust SE inside `ols_rank` and returns it, but the
P7 Monte Carlo coverage test uses the **classical** SE (`se_o`). My independent MC at the same
design (true model = fitted zeta, n = 94) gives OLS coverage:

| SE convention | coverage (mine, R=1000) |
|---|---|
| classical (what the report quotes) | 0.161 (report: 0.158) |
| HC3 | 0.421 |
| HC0 / HC1 | 0.640 / 0.647 |
| MLE, observed-information SE | 0.949 (report: 0.943) |

On the observed data, HC3 = 0.0291 (direct spec) and 0.0328 (inverse spec) — i.e. Ciccone's
reported "robust SE 0.03" is an HC3-type value, not the HC1 the script labels "robust"
(0.0559 / 0.1572). So the *directional* claim survives comfortably — rank-size OLS undercovers
badly at n = 94 under every convention (16–65% vs 95% nominal, against ~95% for the MLE) — but
the quoted "16% vs 94%" mislabels the recipe being criticized by a factor of 3–4 in coverage.

**Proposed correction** (`results/stage1-summary.md` P7 row and the EXT-C1/free-exponent
blocks; receipts gain a per-convention coverage line): state P7 as "OLS nominal-95% coverage
at n = 94 is 0.16 under the classical SE, 0.42 under HC3 and 0.65 under HC0/HC1, against 0.94
for the exact-count zeta MLE — severe undercoverage under every convention; Ciccone's
reported robust SE 0.03 is an HC3-type value on either regression spec."

### F2 — AU-C1's qualifier is imprecise about which route reproduces 47,8; the Tafel-14 numerator is now adjudicated (low–medium)

The four candidate means, recomputed independently:

| route | value | round to 1 dp | truncate to 1 dp |
|---|---|---|---|
| mean of printed A.K., all 94 (sum = 4500) | 47.8723 | 47.9 | **47.8** |
| mean of exact products, all 94 | 47.7540 | **47.8** | 47.7 |
| Tafel numerator 4503/94 | 47.9043 | 47.9 | 47.9 |
| mean of printed A.K., ranks 15–94 | 50.0250 | 50.0 | 50.0 |

So exactly one route yields the printed 47,8 under each convention, and the Tafel's own printed
fraction yields 47,9 under both. Vision at 8× confirms the numerator is **4503**, not 4500 —
the inconsistency is in the Tafel itself, not in the transcription. The quotient 47,8 *is*
consistent with the sum of the printed A.K. column (4500/94 = 47.872) under Auerbach's stated
"abgerundet" convention, which closes deviation 5's "residual curiosity": the Tafel's numerator
and its quotient disagree by one unit in the last digit, and the quotient is the self-consistent
member. The structural finding is unaffected and robust: all-94 means cluster at 47.75–47.90
while the tail mean is 49.89–50.03 (gap 2.15 vs within-group spread 0.15), so 47,8 is an
all-94 statistic and the prose's "tail mean" framing remains a mis-statement.

**Proposed correction** (`results/stage1-summary.md` AU-C1 row; `data/derived/…tafel14…csv`
note; close deviation 5 as adjudicated): "the printed 47,8 reproduces as the all-94 mean under
truncation of the printed-column sum (4500/94 = 47.872 → 47,8, his stated convention) or under
rounding of the exact-product mean (47.754 → 47,8); the Tafel's own numerator 4503 gives 47.904
under either convention and is the inconsistent member of the annotation."

### F3 — EXT-C1: the inverse-spec reading verifies, but "dissolves the F4/D1b tension" overstates it (low)

Independently confirmed: inverse OLS (log rank on log size) slope −1.1489 (implied ξ = 0.8704),
direct OLS ξ = 0.8553, r² = 0.9827, and the identity inverse-slope = r²/direct-slope holds
exactly — the point estimate match to Ciccone's −1.15 is real and the inverse-spec reading is
the best available explanation. Two qualifications:

1. The reported robust SE 0.03 matches HC3 on **both** specs (0.0291 direct, 0.0328 inverse),
   so the SE cannot discriminate them; only the point estimate does. The axis-orientation flag
   (EPB Fig. 4 not on disk) is correctly retained and unchanged.
2. ξ = 0.8704 with an HC3-based 95% interval [0.824, 0.922] sits at/below the **lower** edge of
   the band-implied [0.911, 1.089] (overlap only [0.911, 0.922]). The reading therefore moves
   the tension from above the window to below it; it does not dissolve it. What dissolves it is
   P7/F1: rank-size OLS is not a reliable exponent estimator at n = 94, and the MLE (0.980) is
   the estimate that sits inside the band window.

**Proposed correction** (`results/stage1-summary.md` EXT-C1 row): replace "the F4/D1b tension
dissolves" with "the −1.15 is re-identified as a ζ (= 1/ξ) estimate, ξ ≈ 0.87; the residual
gap to the band-implied window is an OLS small-sample artefact quantified in P7, and the
estimate that sits inside the window is the MLE (0.980). The reported robust SE 0.03 is
consistent with HC3 on either spec and does not discriminate them."

### F4 — the two flagged defects: both confirmed as flagged; corrections proposed, approval-gated (low)

**Prereg §3.3 (r₀ rule) — degenerate as specified, not mis-implemented.** The rule ("smallest
r₀ such that the running tail-mean over [r₀, 94] stays within ±2% of M(r₀) for all r ≥ r₀") is
self-referential: for a series whose tail mean drifts 47.9 → 50.4 over ranks 1–30 it can only be
satisfied where the window is nearly empty (r₀ = 92, n = 3). I also tested the obvious patch —
an increment rule (|M(r+1) − M(r)|/M(r) ≤ tol for all r ≥ r₀, tol ∈ {0.2%, 0.3%, 0.5%}) — and it
degenerates identically (vacuously true at r₀ = 93). Patching the rule family is therefore not
the fix. **Recommendation:** amend §3.3 to make Auerbach's own band-containment criterion
(printed A.K. inside 45–53 for every rank ≥ r₀) the primary operationalization — deterministic,
non-vacuous, and it yields r₀ = 15 exactly, his eyeballed value — report the tail-mean curve
M(r) purely descriptively (it is already in the receipts), and delete the ±2% rule. Amendment
per the prereg §8 format; the prereg itself untouched by this session.

**Claim-inventory AU-C2 parenthetical — wrong as flagged.** Printed A.K. over ranks 1–14 spans
**19–46** (Leipzig 19; Elberfeld-Barmen 46), not 36–53: 36 is Berlin's rank-1 value and 53 first
appears at rank 45, so "36–53" matches neither the printed column nor the exact products
(18.66–46.02). **Recommended replacement text:** "(Ranks 1–14 fluctuate 19–46 — Leipzig 19 at
rank 3, Elberfeld-Barmen 46 at rank 13; the band's upper edge 53 first appears at rank 45.)"
